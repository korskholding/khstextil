Quality and materials

Textiles built for constant use.

Hotel textiles are washed often and in large volumes. That is why we check the material, density, weight and finishing of every item.

Economy – plain weave 140 TC
100% cotton, 160 g/m², sanforized. A reliable choice for everyday use.
Lux – damask 250 TC
100% cotton, mercerized and sanforized. Smoother surface and a subtle sheen, plain or with a 4, 10 or 20 mm stripe.
Lux stripe – damask 250 TC
80% cotton / 20% polyester, 10 mm stripe. A cotton feel with greater strength.
Terry
100% combed cotton, double low loops, 450 / 500 / 550 g/m². Higher weight = denser, more absorbent towel.
Fillings
Microfibre cover, filled with hollow siliconised polyester fibre or fibre balls. Easy to wash and quick to dry.
Short glossary
TC (thread count)
Number of threads per square inch. Higher number = denser, smoother fabric.
Sanforization
A finish that limits shrinkage after washing. Linen keeps its size.
Mercerization
A cotton treatment for greater strength and a subtle sheen.
Damask / sateen
A weave with a smooth, lustrous surface; typical of hotel stripes.
Weight (g/m²)
Fabric weight; for terry it indicates density and absorbency.

We confirm the exact specifications of every item in our quote.

Care – simple rules for a longer life
  • Wash new textiles before first use.
  • Wash white linen separately from coloured items.
  • Don’t overload the washer or dryer – textiles come out cleaner and crease less.
  • Dose detergent according to water hardness; use bleach sparingly.
  • Don’t iron terry, and don’t use fabric softener with every wash – it reduces absorbency.
  • Choose the temperature and method according to the material composition; we’ll send recommendations for specific items with our quote.

Hotel vs. regular retail textiles

Regular textiles are made for occasional washing, and their quality, weight or density are often not stated. Hotel textiles are built for frequent and industrial washing – and you can get the same models a year later.
